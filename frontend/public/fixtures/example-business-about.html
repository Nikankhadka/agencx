About us

Northside Repairs has served the same suburb for fifteen years. We are a family business, and the technician who diagnoses your device is the one who repairs it.

How we work

We explain the fault before we start, confirm the price, and never replace a part that does not need replacing. Every repair carries a ninety day warranty covering the part we fitted and the work we did.

Our promise

If a repair is not ready when we promised, we will call you before you travel. If you are not happy with the work, bring it back and we will make it right.
