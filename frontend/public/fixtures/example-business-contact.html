Contact

Call us on 555-0100, email hello@northsiderepairs.example, or visit 12 High Street. The shop is a short walk from the Northside train station, and there is parking behind the building.

Opening hours

Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm. Saturday, 10am to 2pm. Closed Sunday and public holidays. Weekend drop-offs are welcome, and we will message you when the repair is ready to collect.

Before you visit

Bring the device and any charger or case that belongs with it. If the repair needs a part we do not keep in stock, we will order it and call you with a collection date.
