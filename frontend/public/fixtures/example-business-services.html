Services

Our technicians repair phones, tablets and laptops. Every repair is tested before it goes back to you, and every repair carries a ninety day warranty.

Repairs we do

  • Screen replacement for phone and tablet glass
  • Battery replacement, including same-day fitting
  • Charging port and cable connector repair
  • Laptop keyboard, hinge and screen replacement
  • Water damage assessment and board cleaning
  • Data recovery from phones and laptops

Turnaround

Most screen and battery repairs are finished the same day. A laptop repair that needs a part ordered usually takes two to three working days, and we will call you as soon as the part arrives.
