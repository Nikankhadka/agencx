Northside Repairs

We are a phone and laptop repair shop on the north side. Walk-ins are welcome and most screen repairs are done the same day. Bring the device in, or send us a message describing the fault, and we will tell you what it needs before you travel.

What we do

  • Screen replacements for phones and tablets
  • Battery replacements
  • Water damage assessment and cleaning
  • Laptop keyboard and charge port repairs

Getting a repair booked

No appointment is needed for a diagnostic. We will confirm the price before any work starts, and we will message you when the repair is ready to collect.
