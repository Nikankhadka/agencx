Northside Repairs

We are a phone and laptop repair shop on the north side. Walk-ins are welcome and most screen repairs are done the same day.

What we do

  • Screen replacements for phones and tablets
  • Battery replacements
  • Water damage assessment and cleaning
  • Laptop keyboard and charge port repairs

Opening hours

Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm. Saturday, 10am to 2pm. Closed Sunday.

Getting a repair booked

Bring the device in, or send us a message describing the fault and we will tell you what it needs before you travel.
